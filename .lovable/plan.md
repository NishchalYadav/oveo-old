

# Ovéo — The Surgical Digital System

## Overview
A multi-page, high-fidelity agency website for Ovéo with a stark black/white surgical aesthetic, interactive Three.js hero, and Apple-style navigation. The site will position Ovéo as a precision-first web architecture agency.

---

## Pages

### 1. Home (Hero + Overview)
- **Hero Section**: Full-viewport midnight black canvas with an interactive Three.js glass orb at center — featuring light refractions, fluid rotation responding to mouse velocity
- **Tagline & positioning copy** in geometric monospace font (JetBrains Mono) against the void background
- **Quick overview** of the four services as minimal cards with clinical white borders
- **CTA** directing to the Engage (contact) page

### 2. Services
- Four service sections with detailed descriptions, each on its own visual "panel":
  - **Structural Assembly** — Web development
  - **Surgical Interface** — UI/UX design
  - **Identity Resonance** — Brand development
  - **Operational Intelligence** — Maintenance & lead-gen
- No public pricing; each service card includes a "Request Quote" CTA linking to the Engage form

### 3. Work (Portfolio)
- Grid layout showcasing placeholder project cards with mock thumbnails, titles, and categories
- Designed for easy content replacement later
- Hover effects revealing project details

### 4. About
- Nishchal Yadav's profile and philosophy section
- The "Surgical" methodology explained — why precision over aesthetics
- Agency values and approach

### 5. Engage (Contact)
- High-fidelity contact form with fields: Name, Email, Project Type, Budget Range (in ₹ INR), Message
- Form UI built and validated, ready for future Resend (email) and SteinAPI (spreadsheet logging) integration
- "Custom quote" flow — no pricing displayed, just the engagement form

---

## Design System

- **Background**: #000000 (absolute black)
- **Text/Elements**: #FFFFFF (clinical white)
- **Subtle depth**: #1A1A1A (slate gray for grid lines, borders, hover states)
- **Headings**: JetBrains Mono (geometric monospace)
- **Body**: Inter (clean sans-serif with precise tracking)
- **Grid**: Visible 12-column architectural grid anchoring all layout elements

## Navigation
- Floating Apple-style nav bar with bubble-shaped hover effects
- Links: Home, Services, Work, About, Engage
- Minimal, stays out of the way but always accessible

## Interactions & Animations
- Three.js interactive glass orb on the hero (mouse-responsive rotation and refraction)
- "Surgical cut" page transitions — instant, zero-bloat crossfades
- Subtle hover states on all interactive elements
- Scroll-triggered fade-in animations for content sections
